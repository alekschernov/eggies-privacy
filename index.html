Eggies Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Eggies is a simple game for young children. This page explains what the app does with information. The short answer is that it does not collect any.

Information we collect

None. Eggies does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, and the app never asks for a name, email address, location, photos, or contacts.

What is saved on the device

The friends hatched in the game are saved only on the device the game is played on. This list never leaves the device. Deleting the app removes it.

Ads, purchases, and tracking

Eggies has no ads, no in-app purchases, and no analytics or tracking tools. It does not use any third-party services that collect data.

Internet use

Eggies works without an internet connection and does not send any information over the internet.

Children's privacy

Eggies is designed for children. Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to alekasoft@gmail.com.
